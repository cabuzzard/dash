## Rules

- **insertion** — The AI evaluates an idea against all forms and inserts it non-destructively at the single best position. Manual rearrangement happens via the keyword section in hub cards.
- **holding** — An idea that is not inserted becomes an unclustered topic inside a hub, or a title with 'idea' status unattached to any hub. Held items are isolated from publication and SEO strategy.
- **new-hub** — Creating a hub is a human call today. Target rule: compulsion approx CPC value + total search volume, compared against existing clusters.
- **research-dependency** — Research pulls all upstream keywords plus the input at its level and matches them to market and customer queries. Any insertion at a research-tied form triggers a research update for that insertion.
- **semi-dominance** — The keyword flow is semi-dominant: insertions and research updates are non-destructive and never negate the upstream SEO strategy.
- **internet-search** — Individual titles and products are also market-researched directly from the internet, independently of keywords, then reconciled with SEO.
- **pillar** — One pillar per title, written to the title, long and verbose enough to inform all downstream assets. Pillars are never published or deployed. 'Pillar' appears nowhere in asset creation; assets draw from the pillar in the title only.
- **interview** — An interview is optional and never blocks a pillar. A pillar written without an interview is flagged 'for interview' on the title. The interview humanizes the content and teaches Voice.
- **voice** — Voice is learned from the operator's interview responses. Voice decides HOW content is written; research decides WHAT it says.
- **method** — Method is a singular governing database. A method organizes upstream information into a platform- and intent-specific form; that form is the asset type. A method is descriptive and instructive and stores no asset fields; the asset record stores the fields.
- **strategy** — Strategy is a singular governing table, downstream of Method. It sets periodicity, repetition, schedule, iterations, and topical arcs, and routes productions to the asset class matching their parent type. It is informed by parents through Method; it never informs parents.
- **parents-children** — Parent assets are never published and carry intent (SEO, topical engagement, direct sales). Child assets are publishable instances tied to a parent, inheriting its intent and universal datasets. Direct assets come off the pillar with no parent asset and serve brand/profile growth, not product sales.
- **model-agnostic-enforcement** — Enforcement lives in the tool, not in any model. The flow binds every input regardless of which AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, any other) or human produced it, because every write passes the worker's validation. Models produce drafts and proposals; they never hold unvalidated write access.
- **refusal-and-repair** — A write the worker refuses is never destroyed: it is routed to holding with its full payload and the refusal reason recorded. Repair = fix the data or the modal that produced it, then replay the held write. Nothing is dropped silently.
- **filter-doctrine** — AI (Claude, Buzz) is a FILTER, not a source. It may present opinions as options to edit the source; it never originates changes to the source. Sources: this flow, the hub cards, research, the operator's decisions. Outputs: Notion and the publishing platforms.
