Lead Tracker

For owners & small teams

One list for every inquiry — with an owner, a status, and a next step on every row. No lead dies in the inbox, even when inquiries come in from more than one place.

Demo — read this first: Anything you type in “Add a lead” previews locally on this page only. It saves nothing, sends nothing, and is not visible to the owner. The real client version writes to Notion — a version where this page itself saves leads would need a backend.

How to use it

One row per inquiry. Set the source and owner when the lead comes in, and always fill in the next step — that one field is what keeps a lead from stalling. Move the status forward as you work it:

New→ Contacted→ Qualified→ Wonor Lost

What a complete row has

  • NameThe person or business inquiring — one row per inquiry.
  • SourceWebsite, Referral, Instagram, Google — so you can see which channels actually produce work.
  • OwnerOne named person responsible for the next move. No shared “someone should reply.”
  • StatusNew → Contacted → Qualified → Won or Lost.
  • Next stepThe single next action, written plainly enough to act on without re-reading the whole thread.
