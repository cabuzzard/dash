Deliverable 4 · Already built — in the dash

Client Interview App

The one that already exists — not a new demo. It's the phone-first interview queue in the dash (interviews/, sharing the dash login). It works one Flagged interview at a time: a hub filter up top, the title and angle, why it's being asked, then one textarea per question — “say it the way you'd say it.” Save draft anytime; ✓ Finish & next clears the queue one interview at a time.

Under the hood it runs on one interview per main offering (product), stored in the Notion 🎤 Interviews DB through the worker (getInterviews / saveInterview). An asset that needs its own interview gets one via “🎤 Request interview” inside the asset. It also carries the 🔎 Expert check, which tests each answer's claims against the current standard of practice in the field — with sources, the smallest fix, and the line a top practitioner would add.

  • InputsYour answers, in your own words, to auto-generated questions per product / title — flagged automatically for new titles (within ~30 min)
  • OutputsA Finished interview flows to every title and asset under that offering — it feeds the writers and the 🗣 Your voice engine, so it teaches both what to say and how you say it
  • Status flowFlagged (waiting on you) → Finished. The hub's weekly touch is just: read the queue, answer what's flagged

Why it belongs in the suite: the other three capture what a business sells and schedules. This one captures what the owner knows and sounds like — it's the input that makes every content output sound human instead of generated.
