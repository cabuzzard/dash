# Short video guide (operator research, 26 Nov 2026)

Solo B2B consultant · phone camera · office · LinkedIn 60–120 s. The operator's full research guide (sources, verified-live vs index) lives with the operator; this file keeps the rules the tool uses.

## Order of operations
Background first → audio → light → stability. The camera is last.

## What the tool uses

### Beat map (every script, brief section 8)
| Beat | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| hook | 0:00–0:03 | on screen with the face, no greeting |
| promise | 0:03–0:08 | the outcome in one sentence |
| credibility | 0:08–0:15 | one line |
| step ×3 | 0:15–0:40 | numbered, verbs up front |
| example | 0:40–0:55 | one concrete before/after |
| cta | 0:55–1:05 | the call to action |

For 90–120 s, extend the value and example sections. Don't add a second topic.

### Captions and layout (Creatomate template)
- Captions are burned in, every time. Proofread names and terms.
- A headline under 8 words sits at the top from the first frame, saying something different from the first spoken line.
- Captions go a little below centre, under the chin. Keep the bottom quarter clear (platform UI).
- Keep essential text inside the centre 80% of the frame.
- Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920) is best for reach. 1:1 and 4:5 are fine for talking heads.
- Export MP4 H.264, 24–30 fps.

### Still images (the Grok backgrounds)
- Don't fake an office behind a person with a still. A real, modest office corner reads as more credible.
- Use stills as faceless caption-video backgrounds, full-frame B-roll inserts (2–4 s), or picture-in-picture.
- Background photos should be calm, with 1–3 objects, darker and softer than the subject or text. No readable text, no bright window, no hotspots.

### Pacing
- Show a visual change every 5–8 s (punch-in, B-roll or text).
- B-roll: film 5–10 s, use 2–4 s. Aim for 5–8 shots per 60–120 s.
- No B-roll over the hook. Come back to the face for the CTA.

## Shooting (talking-head videos)
- **Background:**
  - a real, tidy office corner 3–6 ft behind you;
  - a neutral wall (warm grey, beige, navy, charcoal);
  - 1–3 props: plant, warm lamp, one credible object;
  - light the background a step darker than your face.
- **Audio:** a lav mic a hand's width below the chin. Switch off fridge, AC and fans. Test 10 s with headphones first.
- **Light:**
  - key light 45° to one side, slightly above eye level;
  - fill on the other side at about half strength;
  - a rim light behind;
  - face the window, never put it behind you.
- **Phone:**
  - rear camera at 1x;
  - 4K/30;
  - HDR off;
  - white balance, AE/AF and camera locked;
  - grid on;
  - on a tripod at eye level.
- **Delivery:** hybrid. Hook and CTA word for word, the body from bullets. Start mid-sentence.
- **Weekly batch (60 min, 3 videos):**
  - prep 15 min;
  - set up 10 min;
  - A-roll 15 min;
  - B-roll 10 min;
  - then edit to the beat map.

## Open questions from the research
- B-roll shot counts differ between sources.
- Gear models and prices weren't verified.
- Phone Portrait blur isn't endorsed for talking-head video.
- The LinkedIn claims are practitioner guidance, not LinkedIn's own data.
