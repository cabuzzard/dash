Fractional Construction PM + AI Tools — Without a Full-Time Hire

2026-10-22

You're the PM on every job right now. You know it, and you know it's not sustainable. Schedules live in your head, submittals stack up until they're emergencies, change orders go out late and get argued over, and cost bleed shows up at closeout instead of while you can still recover it. Hiring a full-time senior PM at $90K–$120K doesn't pencil yet. Doing it yourself is capping your growth. This retainer is the answer that lives in the window between those two options — an experienced construction PM embedded in your operation, paired with AI-enabled tooling built specifically for construction coordination workflows, scoped to your actual project count.

Who This Is For

This is built for owner-operators of general contracting, remodeling, and specialty trade companies running between three and fifteen active jobs at once with no in-house PM layer. You have field credibility, real revenue — often $2M to $20M annually — and hard-won trade knowledge. What you don't have is a back-office infrastructure that keeps up with the coordination, documentation, and reporting overhead that scales faster than your crew does. If you're still doing your own submittals, schedule updates, and change orders after regular business hours, you're already past the threshold where this pays for itself.

What's Included

- **Fractional PM oversight** — an experienced construction PM working inside your operation on a defined retainer, not a consultant who drops in once a month and disappears. - **Submittal, RFI, and change order management** — logged, tracked, routed, and followed up in rhythm so nothing stacks into a crisis. - **Schedule management** — master schedule updated from field inputs weekly; lookaheads produced and sequencing conflicts flagged before they hit the field. - **Subcontractor coordination** — written confirmation of schedules, scopes, and coordination requirements with a documentation trail built as work progresses. - **Weekly project reporting** — cost summary against budget with variance flagged, schedule status against baseline, open submittals and RFIs with aging, change order log with current approval status, and an open items list with owners and due dates. Takes you ten minutes to read. Keeps you in control without putting you in every meeting. - **AI-enabled coordination infrastructure** — schedule update generation, document routing, reporting automation, and cost tracking dashboards running in the background, handling the high-volume coordination overhead that kills time without requiring judgment.

How It Works

Onboarding runs two to four weeks. The PM embeds directly into your live jobs — your tools, your sub list, your documentation systems, your actual active work. Not theoretical. Not a template setup. Your current jobs, your current people.

From there, the retainer runs month-to-month on a defined scope: an agreed active project ceiling and a set of recurring deliverables negotiated at onboarding. Adjustments to scope happen in advance, not reactively. You know exactly what's covered and what isn't before the engagement starts.

The AI layer runs the coordination overhead — schedule updates, document routing, reporting cadences, cost tracking dashboards — consistently and without the lag that comes from doing it manually. The PM layer supplies the judgment, the relationship management, and the field-tested decision-making that software cannot replicate. Consequential decisions stay with you. The system that feeds those decisions is current and maintained without you having to hold it together personally.

Why It Works

Coordination overhead doesn't scale with revenue — it scales with project count. Every job you add doesn't just add revenue; it adds a submittal log, an RFI chain, a change-order queue, and a subcontractor schedule that needs managing. At three jobs you absorb it. At seven you're running on memory. At ten your memory is the bottleneck. That's a structural problem, not a willpower problem. The fix is a coordination layer that runs without you having to personally hold it together.

Most alternatives make you choose: hire a full-time PM you can't fully load, buy software you still have to operate yourself, or stay the bottleneck. This retainer collapses that choice. You get the PM and the tooling as a single engagement, calibrated to your actual project count, contractor-side only — no owner's rep conflict, no developer allegiance, just your jobs running tighter.

**"An outsider won't know my jobs."** That's a real concern when the person you're handing off to is a generic VA or an offshore admin.
