Why it works

Decision paralysis lives in the space between bids that were written to win, not to be compared. Bid Check collapses that space into one legible document by extracting scope from every bid against the same matrix — so the gaps that would surface as change orders after mobilization are named and priced before you sign the award.

The objection it answers

If you are thinking you can do this yourself in a spreadsheet, you can — it takes two to four hours you do not have on bid day, and it will miss the gaps that are absent rather than different. The value is not the spreadsheet; it is the trained eye on what is not there.
