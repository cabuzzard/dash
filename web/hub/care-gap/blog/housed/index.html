Housed

2026-10-21

For Direct care workers and home health aides whose rent is one missed shift from trouble. Your household stays stable through the gap — before the eviction, before the job loss, not after.

What's included

Emergency bridge funds applied directly against rent arrears, utility shutoffs, or deposit gaps

Streamlined intake built around a care worker's schedule and non-traditional income — not a corporate HR file

Casework follow-through that maps your household to longer-term subsidy programs without you having to research them alone

Connection to employer housing benefit conversations and workforce retention resources that compound the stabilization

Fast deployment timed to eviction windows — not grant disbursement calendars

Why it works

This program was designed specifically for direct care workers — the income verification, the documentation bar, the timeline — because general housing programs were not built for how care work is actually paid and scheduled, and that design failure is exactly why caregivers keep getting turned away. Every stabilized household is tracked as a workforce retention event, which means the data builds the case for more resources for everyone in your position.

The objection it answers

You have probably been turned away before, and you have learned to assume the answer is no. This one was built for your role specifically — check eligibility before you decide the answer is the same as last time.

Terms

Free to qualifying direct care workers; access through employer or agency referral, direct community outreach, or direct application — no waitlist model that lets the eviction win while paperwork processes
