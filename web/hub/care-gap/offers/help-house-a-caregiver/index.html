Help House a Caregiver

Every dollar you deploy keeps a skilled caregiver housed, employed, and counted — turning a housing crisis into a documented workforce continuity event.

Who it's for: Foundations, impact investors, and CSR funders with housing, workforce, or ESG mandates

What's included

  • Emergency housing intervention grants disbursed directly to caregivers at the moment of crisis — covering rent arrears, security deposits, or utility stabilization, each tied to verified caregiver employment status
  • Stabilization case coordination connecting each recipient to financial coaching, employer-linked benefits navigation, and community housing resources to reduce re-entry risk
  • Per-household outcome reporting delivered to funders as structured data — each stabilized household logged as a workforce continuity event with employment verification, intervention amount, and retention timeline
  • Aggregated impact reporting on a quarterly or annual cadence showing households kept stable, caregivers retained in the field, and cost-per-retained-worker — formatted to satisfy both philanthropic impact mandates and institutional ESG documentation
  • Replicable program infrastructure available to disability organizations, care employers, and regional nonprofits as a co-deployed model with data architecture already configured for ESG reporting cycles and workforce impact dashboards

Why it works

Replacing an experienced direct care worker costs $3,000–$5,000 in recruitment, onboarding, and lost continuity — a targeted housing intervention sized to the specific crisis intercepts that exit at a fraction of that cost. Because eligibility is tied to documented caregiver employment status from intake, every stabilized household is an auditable data point in a workforce retention model, not a one-time charity transaction.

The objection it answers

If you're wondering whether this is just rental assistance with a workforce label on it — it isn't. General emergency rental assistance has no mechanism to track whether the tenant stayed employed; this program ties eligibility to active caregiver status, documents the employment-housing link from intake through retention, and delivers the aggregated proof points your ESG and workforce impact reporting actually require.
