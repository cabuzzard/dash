House a Caregiver

Your gift covers the exact housing shortfall standing between a caregiver and losing their home — so they can stay housed and keep showing up for the person who needs them.

Who it's for: Compassionate donors who want their giving to reach a real person in a real crisis

What's included

  • Direct housing assistance deployed to a verified caregiver in need — rent support, transitional housing, or caregiver accommodation
  • Suggested giving tiers tied to concrete milestones: one week of rent, one month of housing stability, or a full transitional housing placement
  • Impact reporting showing exactly how your funds were used and who they reached
  • Tax-deductible receipt through a registered nonprofit with public financial disclosures and third-party accountability oversight

Why it works

Caregiver housing gaps are often measured in hundreds of dollars — a modest, targeted gift can close the exact shortfall that would otherwise end a caregiving relationship entirely. Because the program runs on a direct-assistance model, your dollars move to a caregiver's shelter with minimal administrative overhead, protecting two people at once: the caregiver you house and the person they care for.

The objection it answers

Most donors wonder whether their money actually reaches a caregiver or disappears into overhead — this program publishes its fund allocation data and sends individual impact reports so you can see the specific outcome your gift created.

Terms

One-time or recurring gifts accepted at any amount; tax-deductible; processed through a registered nonprofit; impact reporting delivered to donors after funds are deployed
