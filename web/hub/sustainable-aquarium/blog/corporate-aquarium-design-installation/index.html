Corporate Aquarium Design & Installation

2026-10-22

A living aquarium is one of the few design features that actively improves with time — but only if it was specified correctly from day one. This is a practitioner-led design, installation, and care-planning service for commercial interiors: offices, hotel lobbies, restaurant environments, reception areas, and hospitality spaces where first impressions carry real weight. No off-the-shelf configurations. No cookie-cutter product line. Every installation is built around the biology of your specific site.

Who This Is For

Interior designers, facilities managers, hospitality directors, and commercial property owners who have the design brief and the budget authority — but no reliable framework for evaluating what a commercial aquarium installation actually involves. You want something that reads as considered, not decorative. You are not a hobbyist, and you are not going to manage it yourself. You need a vendor who can specify the system, coordinate the installation, and hand you a care plan that makes operational sense for a high-visibility environment.

If you have ever looked at an aquarium in a commercial space and thought 'that looks abandoned' — you already understand the problem this service solves.

What's Included

- **Site assessment** — physical constraints established before any design work begins: light levels, floor loading, HVAC proximity, maintenance access, traffic patterns, and who is on-site long-term - **Written specification** — design concept, equipment list, substrate and plant selection, installation scope, and project cost delivered as a single document before any commitment is made - **Installation coordination** — tank construction or procurement, substrate layering, initial planting, water conditioning, and livestock introduction managed in sequence - **Care plan** — documented maintenance protocol specifying what the system needs, at what frequency, and what early indicators of imbalance look like; delivered as part of the project, not as an afterthought - **One point of contact** — from site assessment through installation handoff; no handoff chain between a decorator, a plumber, and a pet store

The assessment fee is credited against the full project. Full project pricing is quoted after assessment — scope and complexity vary by site.

How It Works

Engagement begins with a paid site assessment. That visit establishes what is actually viable in your space — not what looks viable from a floor plan or a room description. Light environment, structural constraints, access logistics, and the realistic maintenance profile for your team are all established on-site before the specification is written.

The specification that follows covers everything: design concept, equipment rationale, plant and substrate selection, installation sequence, and a fixed project cost. Procurement and facilities teams get what they need to evaluate the project against budget before any money moves beyond the assessment. Installation is then coordinated end-to-end. The care plan is delivered at handoff — not as a manual, but as a practical document that tells anyone on your team what to look for and when to call.

Why It Works

Most commercial aquariums are over-engineered and under-biologically-understood. They depend on mechanical intervention — large filtration systems, CO2 injection, scheduled chemical treatments — to hold stability. When that intervention lapses, the system degrades visibly and quickly. A natural planted system built on Walstad-method substrate biology works differently: nutrient cycling is driven by plant roots and living soil, not equipment cycles. The system is stable between visits because the maintenance is being done by the biology, not the schedule. That is a different failure profile. A missed maintenance visit is an inconvenience, not a crisis.

In a high-visibility commercial environment — a hotel lobby, a corporate reception, a restaurant dining room — that distinction is the difference between a feature that enhances the space and one that undermines it. The biology is specified correctly at the outset, or it is not. There is no retrofit.

The Objection This Answers

**'We don't know what happens after you leave — or what it costs to find out.'**

Both questions are answered before any commitment is made. The site assessment produces a written specification that includes a complete care plan: what the system needs, how often, and what an early problem looks like before it becomes visible. The maintenance footprint of a natural planted system is lower than a conventional commercial aquarium — the biological stability is baked into the substrate from day one, not delivered by a service technician every three weeks. And the project cost is fixed in the specification document, so there is no ambiguity in procurement. The assessment fee is credited against the full project. You are not paying to be sold to — you are paying for a real specification.
