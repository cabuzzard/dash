Estate Care Consulting

Turn a high-value property that runs on memory and luck into a documented system that holds up whether you're home, traveling, or handing the keys to the next generation.

Who it's for: Affluent homeowners in Danville, Alamo, Blackhawk, and comparable Northern California communities who own complex properties and want the infrastructure to run them — not just someone to run them for you

What's included

  • Estate Readiness Assessment (Sessions 1–2): a structured on-site or virtual property audit that surfaces your risk exposure, vendor dependencies, and absentee vulnerabilities — delivered as a prioritized findings map you keep
  • Playbook Development (Sessions 3–5): a guided build-out of your core management infrastructure — vendor hierarchy, emergency protocols, seasonal maintenance schedule, and absentee operating procedures — formatted as a living document you own and update
  • Succession & Handoff Module (Session 6): institutional knowledge documented in transferable form, ready for family, staff, or a future estate manager — no knowledge-transfer meeting required
  • Direct coach access between sessions for clarifying questions during the active engagement
  • All frameworks, checklists, and protocols delivered as owner-held documents — not locked in a platform, not dependent on continued engagement to remain useful

Why it works

Owners who complete a full estate risk assessment routinely surface 8–15 previously untracked vulnerabilities before any of them become a crisis — deferred maintenance items, undocumented vendor dependencies, emergency gaps that only appear when you're four time zones away. The frameworks behind this coaching have been tested against real absentee scenarios — wildfire evacuation windows, caretaker departures mid-season, extended international travel — and they're the same operational infrastructure professional estate caretakers rely on in the field.

The objection it answers

'I already have a property manager or caretaker who handles this.' A person isn't a system — when they leave, get sick, or go dark during an emergency, what they knew leaves with them. This coaching doesn't replace your people; it documents what they do so the property is never hostage to any one of them.

Terms

Six structured sessions over 8–12 weeks, delivered virtually or on-site in the Danville/Alamo/Blackhawk area. Investment available upon inquiry; scaled for single-property and multi-property engagements. Guarantee: if you don't have a clearer picture of your property's risk exposure and documentation gaps after Session 1 than when you started, the engagement ends there at no further charge.
