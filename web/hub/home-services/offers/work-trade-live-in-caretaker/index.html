Work-Trade Live-In Caretaker

Hand one trusted person the keys and a list, and come home to a lived-in house, cared-for pets, and a shorter repair list — without coordinating four separate people.

Who it's for: Northern California homeowners leaving for a weekend to several months

What's included

  • Live-in presence for the full duration — house is occupied, secure, and watched
  • Daily pet care following your written routine (dogs, cats, fish, small livestock, aquariums)
  • Yard, plant, and mail upkeep so nothing goes feral or piles up
  • Sub-threshold handyman repairs and yard projects worked through during the stay (work-trade or mix option)
  • Written scope agreed before arrival — pet routine, property tasks, repair list, escalation contacts

Why it works

One experienced person with real handyman skills lives in rather than dropping by, so problems get noticed and handled the day they happen — not discovered when you get home. References, a background check, and a written scope before any keys change hands mean you're not betting on an app profile.

The objection it answers

Work-for-stay sounds complicated — it's one written scope agreed before the stay begins, same as any simple contract, with repairs priced transparently against the nightly rate. Only no-permit, sub-threshold work is in scope; anything bigger gets flagged for a licensed contractor.

Terms

Three structures: (1) Paid — nightly or weekly rate for house, pet, and light property care. (2) Work-trade — the stay exchanged for an agreed repair and yard scope, valued against the nightly rate. (3) Mix — reduced rate plus a written work list. Longer stays and repeat bookings get better terms. Rates set by scope, dates, and location — contact to get a written estimate.
