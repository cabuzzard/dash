Rental Property Care & Owner Support — Local Oversight

2026-10-22

You own a rental property in Northern California. You're not next door to it. You're managing the listing yourself — or you've patched something together — and you already know the gap: nobody local you can actually call when something needs eyes, when a turnover gets missed, when a vendor doesn't show. This is the support layer that closes that gap. Not a property manager. Not a cleaning crew. Not a stranger from an app. One person who knows your property, shows up between stays, catches what's slipping, and tells you plainly what they found.

Who This Is For

Vacation home and short-term rental owners in Northern California who self-manage their listing — or use a booking platform — but have no reliable local presence on the ground. Typically one to four units: a second home, an inherited property, a vacation rental generating income you don't want to hand over to a management company. You're a capable owner. You understand how your rental operates. You've just hit the wall where remote ownership without on-the-ground oversight creates real risk, and you know it.

If you live more than an hour from the property, have no single local contact who can both check on the place and handle the small fix when they find something wrong, or you've been cobbling together a handyman, a cleaning crew, and a neighbor with no overlap between them — this is the arrangement you've been looking for.

What's Included

- Scheduled condition checks — documented walkthroughs between guest stays or on an agreed standing frequency; notes and photos, not a filtered summary - Turnover readiness walkthroughs — a physical walk of the unit after the clean and before the next arrival to catch what falls outside the cleaning crew's scope; small fixes handled on the spot - Small unlicensed repairs — jobs under the California sub-threshold (roughly $1,000, no permit required) that licensed contractors won't take; handled instead of left on a list - Vendor coordination — when a licensed trade is needed, the right person gets contacted, met on-site, confirmed, and reported back on; you don't chase anyone - Straightforward owner reporting — what was checked, what was found, what was handled, what's pending; readable in five minutes, no surprises buried in it

How It Works

Engagement is scoped per property. At the start, we review what your property needs, how it's being used, and how often it needs attention. From there, an agreed service package is set — some combination of condition checks, maintenance coordination, small repairs, turnover walkthroughs, and reporting — with frequency and depth defined upfront so there are no surprises on scope or cost.

Ongoing coordination happens by phone, text, or email — whatever you actually use. You get reports when something happens and scheduled reports on the agreed cadence. When something comes up that needs a licensed trade or exceeds what can be handled immediately, you hear about it before it becomes a guest problem.

Work-trade arrangements are available for owners who have a skill, material, or resource worth exchanging rather than straight cash. It's a negotiation like any other, with the same clear terms. If cash is simpler, cash works fine.

Why It Works

The problem with remote rental ownership isn't usually the big things — it's the accumulation of small things nobody caught. A burned-out light. A sticky lock. A turnover that was rushed. A vendor who said they'd show and didn't. These compound quietly until a guest checks in and sends you a message, or you drive out two months later and see what's been sitting there.

What changes with a local caretaker who already knows the property is the timing. Problems get caught during a routine check instead of after a guest complaint. Small repairs get done when they're small instead of after they've become expensive. The turnover is confirmed by someone physically present, not assumed based on a cleaning checklist. And you have one person's number — someone who knows the gate latch, knows where the shutoff is, and is already accountable for the place.

The Main Objection Answered

"Isn't this just property management? Are you licensed for that?"

No — and that's stated plainly up front. Regulated property management activities — leasing, tenant screening, rent collection, legal compliance — are excluded from this scope unless lawfully authorized. What's offered is property care and operational support: condition checks, small unlicensed repairs, vendor coordination, and turnover readiness. The line between property care and regulated management is clear, it's written into the scope, and it's respected. If you're looking for someone to run your listing and collect rent on your behalf, this isn't that. If you're looking for a capable local person to watch your property and keep it in order while you run it yourself, this is exactly that.

Terms and Next Step

Pricing is flat or per-visit depending on the agreed service package — no percentage of rent, no leasing services. Work-trade arrangements are negotiated directly. Operating area is Northern California. Exact pricing and schedule are set during the initial inquiry.

Regulated activities (leasing, rent collection, tenant placement) are excluded unless lawfully authorized. Scope is defined in writing before any work begins.
