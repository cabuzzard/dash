Live-In Pet Care

2026-10-21

For Northern California homeowners with pets who travel and have run out of people they actually trust. Your pet stays home, on its routine, with one known person in the house — you leave without dread and come back to an animal that's fine.

What's included

Pre-trip walkthrough to build a written care plan — feeding times, walk schedule, medications, house quirks, emergency contacts, vet protocol

Live-in presence for the full duration: sitter sleeps in the home, keeps the pet's existing routine, and is there morning and night

Agreed check-in cadence so you know what's happening without having to ask

Basic house oversight — mail, lights, deliveries, and eyes on anything that starts acting wrong

Minor issue handling or immediate flagging if something comes up mid-stay

Why it works

The routine doesn't break and the house doesn't go dark — that combination is what boarding and drop-in visits both fail to deliver. This runs on referrals, not a platform profile; past clients and their numbers are available before you decide anything.

The objection it answers

You don't know me — and that's exactly the right instinct. This starts with a walkthrough, not a booking: you meet in person, see how the animals respond, and decide if it's a fit before any money moves.

Terms

Flat or daily rate quoted after the pre-trip walkthrough, based on number of animals, length of stay, and any added house-care scope. No platform fees. Work-trade arrangements considered for the right fit.
