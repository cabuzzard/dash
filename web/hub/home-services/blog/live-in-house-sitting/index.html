Live-In House Sitting

2026-10-21

For Northern California homeowners leaving for an extended stretch with a property worth watching. You hand over the key to someone you know, leave, and come back to a house that ran without you.

What's included

Full live-in occupancy for the duration of your absence — not drop-ins, not overnights, an actual resident

Routine home and yard care: watering, trash, mail, packages, lights that change the way a lived-in house looks

Pet care in your home, on their schedule — no kennel, no daily stranger at the door

Small repairs within California's unlicensed handyman threshold handled on the spot, not logged and left

Scheduled owner updates at a frequency you both agreed to before arrival — no news means good news

Why it works

The scope is written down before anyone arrives, so both sides know exactly what's covered and what gets escalated. Referral-sourced, not platform-matched — the chain of trust starts with someone who already handed over their key.

The objection it answers

You don't know me yet — and that's the right hesitation. This arrangement doesn't start with a profile and a rating; it starts with a referral from someone who already left me their house and came back to no surprises.

Terms

Scoped per trip, not a subscription. Pricing is paid, work-trade, or a negotiated mix depending on property, duration, and scope. Terms set by written agreement before arrival. No platform fee, no intermediary.
