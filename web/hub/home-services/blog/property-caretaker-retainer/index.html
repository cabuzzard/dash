Property Caretaker Retainer

2026-10-21

For Rental owners who aren't local and can't afford to find out something's wrong after the fact. Someone who knows your property is on it — small problems caught, turnovers confirmed, owner reports delivered, no surprises.

What's included

Scheduled condition checks with a written owner report documenting what was found

Turnover readiness checks — property confirmed guest- or tenant-ready, not just assumed

Small repairs within California's unlicensed handyman threshold (~$1,000, no permit required)

Maintenance coordination — right contractor identified, scheduled, and overseen so you don't manage it from a distance

Optional guest or tenant contact, scoped in separately if you want it

Why it works

This is one consistent local person who knows your property's quirks — not a rotating app crew, not a cleaning service that doesn't inspect. Referral-built, multi-season track record across Northern California rental properties, with documented condition reports that most caretaking arrangements never produce.

The objection it answers

You already have a cleaning crew — but a cleaning crew turns the unit, they don't inspect it, catch what's broken, or tell you anything. These are different jobs, and only one of them keeps small problems from becoming expensive ones.

Terms

Ongoing retainer or per-visit engagement, scoped to your property's actual needs. Paid, work-trade, or a negotiated mix. Scope and visit frequency set at the start, adjusted as things change. No long-term management contract required. Pricing set at intake conversation.
