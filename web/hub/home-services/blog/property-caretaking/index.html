Property Caretaking

2026-10-21

For Second-home and vacation-property owners in Northern California who are an hour or more away and have no trusted local person on the ground. Your second home gets checked on schedule, problems get caught small, and you stop dreading what you'll find when you pull into the driveway.

What's included

Scheduled in-person property checks — weekly, biweekly, or monthly — with a direct owner report and photos after each visit

Seasonal opening and closing: winterization, plumbing startup, pre-arrival walk-through so the place is actually ready when you arrive

Small in-scope maintenance handled on the spot — hinges, screens, downspouts, the things that sit on a list for months waiting for a contractor who won't call back

Immediate flagging and trade coordination for anything outside legal unlicensed scope — you get a clear answer and a next step, not a workaround

One point of contact who holds your keys, knows your property across seasons, and has your number — not a dispatch line

Why it works

This service runs on word-of-mouth referrals from other owners in the same area — you can talk to people who've used it before you commit to anything. The property knowledge builds across seasons: a caretaker who caught a roof issue in November is worth more than one showing up cold the day something fails.

The objection it answers

Handing your keys to someone you don't know yet is the whole problem — which is why this starts with a fit conversation and references from owners nearby, not a platform profile. If something breaks beyond unlicensed scope, you get an honest answer and coordination with the right trade, not a guess.

Terms

Per-visit pricing; visit rate reflects Northern California rural and semi-rural service area with travel time factored in. Seasonal opening and closing available standalone or bundled with a check schedule. Small maintenance billed hourly during or between visits. Work-trade arrangements available for the right fit. No long-term contract required. Starts with a fit conversation to agree property, dates, duties, and arrangements before any written scope is signed.
