I Come to You

2026-10-21

For Northern California homeowners who travel or own more than they can manage from a distance. You leave. One person lives there, handles the list, and the property is actually in someone's hands.

What's included

Live-in presence on your property for the agreed dates — continuous, not scheduled drop-ins

Small repairs under the California unlicensed threshold knocked off the list during the stay

Yard work, pet care, and property oversight under one agreement, not sourced separately

Written scope before anything starts — duties, hours per week, expenses, and any cash component named in advance

Handover at the end with documented condition and any follow-up flagged

Why it works

No sitting app offers a handyman and no handyman offers live-in caretaking — this arrangement exists in the gap between both, structured as a clear written exchange so there is no ambiguity about what was promised. Past homeowners are available as references; repeat arrangements with the same owners are the plainest proof it works.

The objection it answers

You do not know me — and that is exactly the right place to start. References from past homeowners are available before any agreement is made, and the written scope exists to make trust concrete before you hand over the keys.

Terms

Exchange of accommodation for agreed duties; scope, dates, and any cash component fixed in writing before the arrangement starts. Duration is arrangement-specific — short travel coverage to extended caretaking. Contact to discuss fit and structure.
