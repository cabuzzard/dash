The Guitar Letter — Guitar & Music Commentary Newsletter

2026-10-22

The Guitar Letter is a free newsletter for people who listen differently. It's not a tips column, not a gear roundup, not a beginner's guide to anything. It's a practitioner writing down what he notices — about tone, about song construction, about the recordings that actually hold up — and sending it to the people who already know what they're listening for but rarely find anyone naming it precisely.

Who It's For

Adult guitar players and serious music listeners, mostly 35 to 65, who grew up with classic rock, blues, and country rock and still treat music as a living practice. You play at home. You follow a handful of artists deeply. You own physical media or at least think about it. You're the one in the room who notices the room mic on a 1973 recording. You can feel when a tone is right before you can explain why technically. You've mostly given up on mainstream music media because it doesn't speak to you anymore — the algorithms reward takes, not craft, and most commentary treats its audience like they just discovered the genre.

If that's where you are, The Guitar Letter is written for you.

What's Included

Each issue contains four things:

- One short personal observation about music or the creative life — the kind of thing a working guitarist notices that nobody else is writing down. - One guitar or recording insight grounded in feel and mechanics — not theory diagrams, not gear porn, but something tactile: how a sound gets made, why a particular mix lands, where the narrative turn in a song actually lives. - One musician recommendation or close commentary — a look at someone worth paying attention to, or a deeper read on someone you already know. - One direct link to the latest recording, session, or video — so you stay connected to new work without hunting for it.

No paywall. No commitment required to start. If it doesn't land in the first read, unsubscribe — no friction.

How It Works

You subscribe. An issue arrives. You read it — it's short by design, written to respect that you do not have time for another content obligation. Something in it lands: a phrase that names what you've been hearing but couldn't articulate, a recording detail that reframes a song you've known for twenty years, a link to a session that sounds like the real thing. You forward it to the one other person in your life who actually listens this way.

That's the mechanism. One read. One thing that lands. Week after week, the relationship between you and the music gets more precise, more felt, and less solitary.

Why It Works

Most music media is optimized for reach, not depth. The Guitar Letter runs the opposite direction: one voice, one point of view, developed issue to issue. The voice behind it plays, records, and engages with the material in real time — this is not commentary from the outside. Every observation is grounded in what it actually feels like to sit with an instrument and try to make something worth listening to.

The YouTube channel is already doing this work in video form. The newsletter extends that conversation into a quieter, more precise register — the place where the observation can take a sentence or two to breathe instead of needing to hold a camera's attention.

The Objection Worth Answering

*"I already follow too many newsletters and don't read most of them."*

This one is short by design. One observation, one insight, one recommendation, one link. It respects that you do not have time for another content obligation. If it doesn't land in the first read, unsubscribe. No friction, no guilt, no re-engagement sequence. The newsletter earns your attention issue by issue or it doesn't.

And if you're wondering whether the voice and ear will match yours: the YouTube channel is already there. Watch a few videos. If the way the music gets talked about matches how you hear it, the newsletter will too. If it doesn't, it's not for you and that's fine.

Terms and Next Step

The Guitar Letter is free. No cost, no commitment, no paywall at entry. Subscribe with your email address and the next issue comes to you directly.

The newsletter is part of the Creative Flow Guitar ecosystem — the same practitioner behind the original backing tracks and weekly sessions. Future issues will connect to new recordings, videos, and occasional product releases, always with the newsletter doing its job first: delivering something real before asking for anything.
