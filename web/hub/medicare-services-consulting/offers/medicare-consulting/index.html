Medicare Consulting

You understand exactly what your coverage costs, what it excludes, and why — before anyone asks you to choose anything.

Who it's for: Adults 60–75 approaching or questioning Medicare enrollment who refuse to make a multi-year decision inside a sales call

What's included

  • Plain-language breakdown of how Part D, Medigap, and Medicare Advantage actually function — including what each costs you over time, not just the monthly premium
  • Full disclosure of how advisors in this industry get paid, so you know what is shaping the recommendations you have already received
  • Enrollment window review — what deadlines apply to your situation and what missing them costs you permanently
  • A value-based plan match built on your medications, your doctors, and your financial priorities — not on carrier compensation schedules
  • Ongoing access when your situation changes: new prescription, late penalty question, secondary payer issue, annual formulary shift

Why it works

The mechanism behind most bad Medicare decisions is not ignorance — it is making a choice while still inside a fog of commission-shaped advice and carrier-generated marketing. This consulting separates the education from the transaction entirely: no carrier relationships, no enrollment-season pressure, no opaque incentives running underneath the conversation. You decide when you understand, not when someone else's clock runs out.

The objection it answers

Every advisor you have talked to said they were independent. The difference here is structural, not a claim: fee-transparent means the compensation model is explained to you on the first call, before any plan is discussed — and there are no carrier commissions distorting what comes after it.
