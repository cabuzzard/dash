Multifamily Investment Opportunities — Analyzed, Packaged Deals for Qualified Buyers

2026-10-22

You know what you want to buy. You have the capital, the criteria, and the operational capacity to close. What you don't have is a pipeline of deals that actually fit — properties sourced against your buy box, analyzed before they reach you, with the investment thesis already laid out. That's what this is. Not brokerage. Not a listing portal. Not another feed of raw inventory you have to filter yourself. Packaged acquisition opportunities: sourced, underwritten, and delivered matched to criteria you defined.

Who This Is For

Active investors, acquisition firms, family offices, syndicators, and operators who are under real pressure to deploy capital and are not finding enough qualified deal flow to do it. You are not browsing — you have a buy box, return thresholds, and a cost to every week that passes without a deal worth looking at. You've been through the broker circuit. You've scrolled CoStar. You know the listed deals are overrun before you even open the OM. What you need is someone sourcing against your criteria and packaging the analysis before it hits your desk.

What's Included

Every packaged opportunity delivered to you includes:

- Financial analysis built on actual in-place income — net operating income, debt service modeling, and return metrics run under base, conservative, and stress scenarios, not on the seller's pro forma projections - Comparable sales from the specific submarket — not metro-wide averages, not directional estimates; comps pulled and benchmarked against the actual asset - Identified risk factors — deferred maintenance flags, rent roll gaps, vacancy drivers, and anything that changes the underwriting story - Value-add and repositioning thesis — where the upside lives, what it costs to unlock, how long it takes, and what the exit looks like when it's done - A recommended acquisition strategy — how to approach the deal given the thesis, the seller's situation, and current market conditions

Nothing gets sent that doesn't fit your buy box. A narrow set of criteria means fewer packages, not mismatched volume.

How It Works

You submit your acquisition criteria once: asset class, target markets, deal size, return thresholds, financing assumptions, and hold period. That buy box becomes the filter. Properties are sourced against it — off-market, below-market, distressed, value-add, and motivated-seller situations that have not been formally listed or have not run a full marketing process. Every sourced property is screened against your criteria before any analysis begins. What passes gets packaged: AI-assisted financial modeling, comp analysis, risk documentation, and a written investment thesis. The packaged opportunity lands in front of you ready to verify, challenge, or act on. The sourcing and initial underwriting work lives outside your operation. Your team's time goes to the decision, not the digging.

Why It Works

Every other sourcing channel — brokers, LoopNet, CoStar, buyer networks — delivers raw inventory and leaves the underwriting to you. The first filter is always yours, which means your team is spending hours on deals that fail that filter after the fact. This inverts the sequence. The buy box is applied before sourcing begins, not after. AI-assisted analysis means the financial work is completed at a speed and cost no traditional acquisition associate can match, and the output is a documented model — disclosed assumptions, disclosed methodology — not a pitch you have to trust. For buyers in overheated listed markets, this is the only sourcing system purpose-built to find and pre-package off-market and value-add commercial deals against specific acquisition criteria rather than general market exposure.

The Objection Worth Answering

"I already have broker relationships."

Brokers work their own inventory and their own buyer relationships. They send you deals that fit their book, not your criteria — and they send the same deals to the other forty groups on their list at the same time. This is not brokerage. It sources against criteria you submit, packages the analysis before the deal reaches you, and delivers an investment thesis rather than a listing link. No broker does that as a deliverable. The two are not substitutes; they operate in different lanes. Your broker relationships stay where they are. This fills the gap they don't cover: off-market sourcing and pre-packaged underwriting matched to what you've already told the market you want to buy.

Terms and Next Step

This is a deal-sourcing and packaging service, not a subscription and not a listing portal. Pricing and transaction compensation are disclosed at intake and structured to comply with applicable real estate licensing requirements — not buried, not vague, disclosed in full before any deal engagement. Delivery is ongoing and criteria-matched; you receive packages as qualified opportunities are sourced and analyzed against your buy box. There is no charge for packages that don't fit, and nothing gets sent that doesn't.

The next step is straightforward: submit your buy box. Asset class, target markets, deal size, return targets, financing assumptions, hold period. That's the intake. From there, sourcing runs against your criteria and packaged opportunities are delivered as they are identified and analyzed. If the criteria are too narrow to source against reliably, that conversation happens upfront — not after weeks of silence.

Capital that is not working has a cost. Submit your criteria and let qualified deal flow come to you already filtered, analyzed, and ready to act on.
