Multifamily Acquisition Analysis — Underwriting & Due Diligence Support for Investors

2026-10-22

Most investors who blow a multifamily acquisition don't get the math wrong. They get the assumptions wrong — and nobody checked them against source documents before money moved. This service exists for the buyer who knows the deal might be real but isn't confident enough in the analysis to act. That gap — between "this looks interesting" and "I'm ready to move" — is exactly where independent acquisition analysis earns its place.

Who This Is For

Capital-disciplined multifamily and commercial real estate buyers who do not have an in-house acquisitions team and cannot afford to find out the assumptions were wrong after closing. That includes independent buyers evaluating their first commercial acquisition, active syndicators screening deals at volume, and portfolio managers who need a clean picture of where opportunity or exposure actually lives in their existing holdings. You are not a beginner browsing. You know what you want to buy. What you need is verified analytical work behind it.

What's Included

Every engagement delivers a defined work product — selected at the time of request based on where you are in the acquisition process:

- **Screening Report** — initial deal viability: does this candidate hold up under the first layer of scrutiny, and is it worth spending more time on? - **Investment Memorandum** — full underwriting and narrative: rent roll analysis, operating expense verification, comp-sourced valuation, submarket context, deal-level model with labeled assumptions, and downside cases for cap rate, vacancy, and rent growth. - **Market Comparison** — comp-based positioning analysis sourced from real closed transactions, not broker-selected comparables. - **Acquisition Shortlist** — a sourced deal pipeline filtered against your specific buy box, including off-market and below-market candidates your competition isn't working because they're still browsing the same listed inventory. - **Portfolio Opportunity Assessment** — existing holdings analyzed for value, risk, or repositioning potential, so you know where the exposure lives before it surfaces as a problem.

Each deliverable is a document. Not a dashboard. Not a data feed you still have to operate. A finished analytical artifact you can act on, present to partners, or take into due diligence with confidence.

How It Works

You submit an analysis request — no retainer, no subscription, no ongoing commitment required. You identify the work product you need and what you're looking at. Independent research and analysis runs against source documents: the actual rent roll, the actual trailing 12-month operating statement, the actual tax records, the actual closed comp transactions. AI-assisted underwriting is used to accelerate the work, then verified against primary data. Every assumption is labeled — confirmed, estimated, or stress-test variable — so you know exactly which number to trust and why. The finished work product is delivered. The decision is yours.

Why It Works

AI-generated underwriting is fast. Verified AI underwriting is what you can actually move on. The difference is whether someone has checked the model's assumptions against source documents or left them running on broker OMs and market averages — which is internally consistent and still wrong in ways you won't discover until due diligence costs start. Labeled assumptions are the actual work product. When you can see what every input is, where it came from, and what happens to the return if it's off by ten percent, you're not guessing. You're underwriting. That's the gap this fills: institutional analytical depth without institutional overhead, headcount, or retainer structure.

The Objection This Answers

**"I can just use AI tools myself."**

You can, and the output will be fast. The risk isn't the speed — it's the confidence. AI underwriting built on unverified inputs gives you a number that looks right because it's internally consistent. Some of those inputs will be estimated from market averages because the model didn't have access to source documents. You won't know which ones unless someone labels them. This service checks the output against source documents and labels every assumption so you know what's real before you act on it. Your broker's underwriting is built to close the deal. This analysis is built to tell you whether you should. Those are not the same work product.

Terms and Next Step

Engagements are structured per work product type. Pricing is per engagement — no retainer, no subscription. Delivery is a document. The process begins with a submitted analysis request describing the asset, the stage you're at, and the work product you need.

No price is published here because engagement scope varies by asset type, deal complexity, and the work product selected. What doesn't vary: the methodology is transparent, the assumptions are labeled, and you know what was checked, what was estimated, and what was stress-tested before anything reaches your desk.

If you're ready to move on a deal but not confident in the analysis behind it — or if you've been sitting on a candidate you can't quite clear — that is the exact problem this is built to solve. Request an analysis.
