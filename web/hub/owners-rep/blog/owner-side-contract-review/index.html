Owner-Side Contract Review

2026-10-21

For Homeowners holding a contract, bid package, pay application, or change order they don't fully trust. Before you sign or approve another dollar, you'll know exactly what the documents say, what's missing, and what to push back on.

What's included

Document-level review of your project materials — contract, scope of work, bids, schedule, pay applications, change orders, or any combination you submit

Owner-side gap analysis: scope ambiguities, missing milestones, payment terms that shift risk onto you, and change order language that removes your leverage

Written findings in plain language — what is real, what is missing, what is misaligned

Prioritized action plan naming what to address first, what to document, and what to push back on before you commit more money

Optional proposal for ongoing advisory if the project needs continued owner-side eyes

Why it works

Build Watcher reads these documents the way an experienced owner's rep reads them — looking for what the contractor will not volunteer. No project stake, no contractor relationship to protect, no percentage billing: the business model is structurally built around your outcome, not the job's size.

The objection it answers

Your architect manages the design and your contractor manages the build — neither is hired to challenge their own pay application or flag a clause that transfers cost risk onto you. That role belongs to nobody in the room until you put someone there.

Terms

Flat fee; engagement scope and fee agreed in writing before any work begins. Delivered remotely — no site visit required. Timeline defined at scope agreement.
