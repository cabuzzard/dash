On Your Side-Ongoing Build Coordination

2026-10-21

For Homeowners building a custom home, major renovation, or owner-builder project — spending serious money with no independent party reading the paperwork on their behalf. You stop being the least-informed person on your own job site — every draw, change order, and open decision lands in front of you with context before you sign anything.

What's included

Live project plan and milestone schedule maintained and updated as the build moves

Budget tracker tied to actual draw requests and approved change orders — so you know where you stand before you approve, not after

Pay application and change order review against original scope, with a written recommendation before anything reaches your signature

Decision log capturing every open owner choice with a deadline and an owner — so delayed decisions stop running the schedule

Coordinated contractor and design-team communication within agreed scope — nothing moves on a verbal, nothing approved without a written record

Regular written progress reports covering schedule, budget position, and decisions due

Why it works

Build Watcher has no financial relationship with any contractor, subcontractor, or design firm on your project — the fee is flat, published by project scale, and signed by you alone, which means every pay application review and change order analysis is done by someone with one agenda in the room: yours. Field-tested across owner-builder projects and custom builds, the process has identified out-of-scope charges before owners signed them and reduced change orders the GC presented as non-negotiable.

The objection it answers

Your GC says they handle all of this — and they do, for the GC. Pay application review and scope change analysis on your behalf are not services a contractor is hired to provide, and catching a padded draw costs them money; those are not the same job.

Terms

Monthly flat fee, tiered by project scale — published, not a percentage of construction cost, not billable hours. An initial setup engagement establishes the baseline plan, budget structure, and coordination process before the monthly service begins. Engagement runs from project mobilization through substantial completion and owner closeout. Exact fee tiers confirmed at scoping. No equity in the project, no referral relationships with any contractor.
