Why it works

The objection it answers

Your architect reviews the drawings and your attorney reviews the contract — but neither of them is hired to level bids, flag underbuilt allowances, or identify the schedule that was built to win your signature rather than survive the first subcontractor conflict. This review covers the gap between those two scopes, which is exactly where change orders are pre-loaded.
