Frequently asked questions

How long do dried cardoon flowers last?

Dried cardoon heads are among the more durable dried botanicals — properly dried and kept out of direct moisture, they hold their structure and color for years. Keep them away from sustained humidity and direct water, and they will outlast most other dried flowers in an arrangement.

Do dried cardoon flowers fade over time?

The color does mellow gradually — the deep purple-to-silver transition that happens as a cardoon head dries is part of the appeal, and that tone is stable over time. Direct sunlight will accelerate fading in any dried botanical, so indirect light or a shaded interior location is the better placement.

What's the difference between cardoon and artichoke flowers?

Cardoon and artichoke are closely related — both are *Cynara* species — and the dried flower heads look similar. Cardoon flowers are typically larger and more elongated, and they are grown primarily for ornamental use rather than food. The dried cardoon head used in arrangements is the unopened or partly opened flower bud, harvested and dried before the petals fully extend.

Can I grow cardoon myself from plant starts?

Cardoon is a perennial in mild climates and well-suited to the East Bay's dry summers. It is a large plant — expect significant spread — and it blooms in its second year.
