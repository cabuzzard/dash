2026-10-21

For Bay Area residents, families with disabled members, and civic donors who want their money to land somewhere they can see. Your contribution funds a specific, named outcome at the only wheelchair-accessible u-pick flower farm in the East Bay — not an operational budget, a real thing you can point to.

What's included

Named giving levels tied to specific farm outcomes — workshop materials, loaner chair days, path markers, permanent signage recognition

Real-time progress tracking against a stated campaign goal and a defined stretch target

Monthly Garden Sustainer tier for recurring donors who want to keep the farm funded beyond a single grant cycle

Impact updates at 30 days and at project completion — so you know what your contribution built

Private farm experience for Founder-level donors ($2,500+), including recognition on permanent on-site signage

Why it works

The farm's accessibility is already built and in use — documented path grades, loaner chairs on site, verified restroom dimensions — which means every dollar expands something real, not something theoretical. There is no other accessible u-pick flower farm in the East Bay, and that void is exactly what this campaign is funded to sustain.

The objection it answers

You have heard 'accessible' before and shown up to a parking lot. This farm publishes surface types, path widths, restroom dimensions, and loaner chair specs because vagueness is the problem they are solving — and each giving tier reports back when its named outcome is complete.

Terms

Tiered donation drive running 30–60 days with a stated goal; giving levels from $25 (Seedling) to $2,500+ (Founder); recurring monthly giving available as Garden Sustainer tier; no price fabricated — see campaign page for current goal and progress
