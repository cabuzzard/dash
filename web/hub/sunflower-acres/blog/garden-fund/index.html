For Local donors, families connected to disability inclusion, and businesses ready to fund something real and verifiable. Your money funds a named piece of infrastructure — a raised bed, a path segment, a loaner chair — and you see it get done.

What's included

Named use of funds tied to your giving tier — one-time, recurring, or sponsor-level

Progress updates showing the specific item your contribution built or maintained

Scoped sponsorship options with stated deliverables: on-site recognition, co-branded materials, and site photography

Full fund-use disclosure before any check is written

Access to visit the farm and see what your money made

Why it works

Sunflower Acres is already operating — step-free paths, seated-height cutting stations, loaner chairs, accessible restrooms, companions free — so your donation maintains and expands infrastructure a real family will use next weekend, not in three years if a program scales. Every tier maps to a line item, not a general pool.

The objection it answers

You've given to 'accessible' things before and the access wasn't real. Sunflower Acres publishes path grades, surface types, restroom dimensions, and loaner chair availability — visit the farm before you commit; it's open.

Terms

One-time gifts at any amount; recurring monthly support at a donor-selected level; business sponsorships at defined tiers with budgets and deliverables to be confirmed. No minimum. Fund-use documentation and progress updates included at every level.
