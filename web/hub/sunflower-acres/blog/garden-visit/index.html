Experience a Garden Without Barriers — Plan Your Visit to Sunflower Acres

2026-10-22

Most flower farms are beautiful from the parking lot. The field starts about thirty feet past where the pavement ends. Sunflower Acres in Concord, California was built the other way around — the path continues from the accessible parking space through the gate and into the sunflower rows without a step, a slope change, or a surface break. Individual visitors, families, and organized groups can book seasonal garden visits and cut their own flowers, pick up bouquets, join a floral design workshop, or reserve the property for a private group outing. The access is structural, not a modification — and the details are published before you ask.

Who This Is For

This is for wheelchair users and ambulatory disabled people who have booked "accessible" venues before and arrived to find a paved strip beside a field they could not enter. It is for the parents, adult children, and caregivers who absorb every logistical variable in advance so that someone else can just enjoy a day out. It is for activity coordinators at senior centers, care facilities, and disability organizations who carry personal liability for the outings they plan and cannot afford to guess on an access claim.

If you have typed "sunflower farm near me" and clicked through three results looking for a real answer about whether you can get into the field — this is for you. If you need surface types, path grades, restroom dimensions, and a companion policy confirmed in writing before you commit a group — this is for you.

What's Included

- Step-free garden access from the accessible parking area through the fields — end to end, not selectively - U-pick cutting in the field, at seated-height cutting stations inside the rows — not at a table near the exit - Ready-made bouquets and dried flower arrangements available for visitors who want something lasting without cutting - Floral design workshops (seasonal; confirm availability and workstation setup through inquiry) - Group bookings for care facilities, senior centers, disability organizations, schools, and private events — with access documentation provided up front before you confirm - Loaner wheelchairs on site, available without advance notice - Free companion admission — the person assisting you is not a line item on the cost

How It Works

The booking process is straightforward. Individual visitors and families can inquire about current seasonal availability, active flowers, and open visit hours. Walk-in availability may exist during open farm hours, but if you are planning around a specific date, specific mobility requirements, or a group of any size, an advance inquiry is the right move.

Group bookings — for care facilities, senior centers, disability organizations, schools, or private events — are handled through direct inquiry rather than an automated form. This is intentional: the first exchange is where access requirements, group size, mobility specs, and accommodation needs are confirmed, so the coordinator and the farm are aligned before anyone travels. The access documentation supplied in that exchange — surface types, path grades and widths, restroom turning radius, loaner chair availability, companion policy — is specific enough to vet the venue without an in-person site visit.

Use the Plan Your Garden Visit form to start an inquiry, check current availability, or open a group booking conversation.

Why It Works

The access at Sunflower Acres is not a welcome statement. It is infrastructure. The paths are step-free because they were built that way — not because a ramp was added near the entrance and the rest of the field left as-is. The cutting stations are at seated height because that is where they were placed, in the rows, not at a modified table near the parking lot. The restroom dimensions are published because a real venue that has solved the problem says so in writing, not in a general "accessibility-friendly" claim.

The specificity of the documentation is itself the trust signal. Surface materials, path grades and widths, restroom turning radius, loaner wheelchair availability, companion admission policy — all of it available before you book. A group coordinator who has previously had to drive out and walk a venue before committing a group can vet Sunflower Acres remotely. That removes the scouting trip, the phone-tag cycle, and the cycle of lowered expectations.

There is one field, and it is the same field for every visitor.

Sunflower Acres publishes measurable specifications — not a general welcome, not an ADA checkbox — because the access is structural and verifiable. Surface types, path grades, restroom turning radius, loaner chairs on site without a request, free companion admission stated explicitly. The documentation is available before you book so you can check it against your actual equipment and requirements, not find out on arrival.

Terms and Next Step

Sunflower Acres is a seasonal operation in Concord, California — approximately 30 minutes east of Oakland and 45 minutes from San Francisco via Highway 4 or Interstate 680. The sunflower season generally runs late spring through early fall, with peak cutting conditions in midsummer; exact bloom timing depends on each year's planting schedule and weather.

Visit admission and group booking rates are confirmed through inquiry — no price is published here because availability, group size, activity programming, and seasonal conditions all factor into the confirmation. There is no accessibility surcharge. Companion admission is free.

To check current availability, confirm access details for your specific needs, or start a group inquiry, use the Plan Your Garden Visit form. For groups arriving by adapted vehicle, routing and parking logistics are part of the pre-visit confirmation — include your vehicle type and group size when you inquire.
