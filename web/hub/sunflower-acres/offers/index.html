    Your contribution funds a specific, named outcome at the only wheelchair-accessible u-pick flower farm in the East Bay — not an operational budget, a real thing you can point to.
  • Into The Field
    You go into the field and cut your own bouquet — not to the edge of the parking lot, not to the halfway point where the gravel starts.
  • Private Farm Day
    A farm outing where everyone is in the field — not watching from the edge.
  • Little Brown Birds
    Color your way through 40 'drab' backyard birds and come out the other side actually knowing which one is which.
  • Book Your Garden Visit
    Your whole group moves through the same fields, cuts flowers at the same stations, and nobody waits at the edge.
  • Independent Living Multi-Session Package
    A complete, working plan for your specific situation — housing, attendant care, benefits, and next steps — built across multiple sessions with a case manager who already knows how these systems actually work.
