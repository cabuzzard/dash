You find out in 20 minutes whether a garden fundraiser pencils out for your org — or you find out it doesn't, which is equally useful.

Who it's for: Nonprofits, school garden programs, and community orgs that need a fundraiser with a real hook

What's included

  • A direct read on whether your setup — garden, partner venue, existing community — can support a garden fundraiser

Why it works

The call starts with what you already have — foot traffic, seasonal blooms, community goodwill, a partner venue — and builds the fundraising structure around those assets, not a generic program dropped on top of them. Sunflower Acres is a working, wheelchair-accessible flower farm with infrastructure already built and visitors already moving through it; that is what the model runs on.

The objection it answers

You already have a fundraiser, or you've done events and they didn't raise much. This call isn't asking you to drop anything — and if past events underperformed, that's a structure problem, not an audience problem. The call looks at your donor base honestly and tells you whether this pencils out before you commit to a single hour of volunteer time.
