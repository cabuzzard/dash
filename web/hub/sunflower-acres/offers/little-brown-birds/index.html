Little Brown Birds

Color your way through 40 'drab' backyard birds and come out the other side actually knowing which one is which.

Who it's for: New backyard birders, relaxed colorers, and gift buyers who want something that teaches without feeling like homework

What's included

  • ~40 pages of accurate line art — one common LBB per page, anatomically honest, ready to color
  • Real color names located on each bird: rufous crown, buff eyebrow, pink legs, olive wing edging — the vocabulary a birder uses
  • A suggested palette per page so you know what to mix and where to put it
  • The single field mark that separates each bird from its nearest lookalike
  • A fact or two per species — the kind that makes you put the pencil down for a second

Why it works

Coloring forces you to find the rufous crown before you can paint it — so by the time you finish a page, you've absorbed the ID, not just looked at it. The lesson is built into the act, not bolted on afterward.

The objection it answers

'Brown birds are boring' — that's exactly the premise the book overturns. By page three you'll know a junco has a slate hood, a pink bill, and white outer tail feathers it flashes when it flies, and you won't call it boring again.
