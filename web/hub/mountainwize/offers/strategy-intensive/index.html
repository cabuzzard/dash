Strategy Intensive

What's included

Why it works

Decision paralysis lives in the theoretical space you keep making bigger — this session collapses it by working from your actual value set outward, the way field conditions force a decision that a spreadsheet never will. The methodology comes from someone who built, lost, and rebuilt under real constraint: no institutional padding, no framework theater, just the mechanics that hold up when a hard quarter hits.

The objection it answers

You've done coaching before and it didn't stick — because most coaching sells you insight without a decision procedure. This session ends with a ranked list and a named next move; insight is a byproduct, not the product.
