The Skill Every AI Implementation Needs: Someone Who Can Run the Agents

Carousel preview — 7 slides — for approval / layout review, not yet published
The businesses getting real results from AI are not just using better tools. They have someone who actually knows how to run them. Prompt engineering got everyone in the door. But configuring an agent correctly — setting its context, defining its tools, locking down permissions, building in memory, and making sure it can catch its own errors — that is a different skill entirely. And it is one most small businesses have not filled yet. This carousel breaks down what proper agent setup actually looks like, why local AI models deserve a spot in your planning conversation, and what the role of an agent operator looks like inside a small team. No technical background required to understand this. Just a willingness to take AI implementation seriously rather than assuming the tool does the work on its own. Save this one. It is a framework worth having in your back pocket before your next planning conversation. Follow for the next post in this series — we're going deeper on how to structure this role inside a small team.
