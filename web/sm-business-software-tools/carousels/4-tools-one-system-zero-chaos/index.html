Most service businesses don't have a productivity problem. They have a tool overlap problem. You've got a scheduling app that doesn't know about your CRM. An invoicing tool disconnected from your payments. A client folder buried somewhere in your downloads. None of it talks. All of it costs you time. In 2025, the only stack a new service business needs is four tools — chosen to work together from day one: → Calendly for booking → HubSpot CRM for client management → Wave for invoicing and payments → Notion for delivery and docs That's it. One system. Every tool hands off to the next. The goal isn't having the right tools. It's having tools that form a system — so your business runs even when you're not actively running it. Save this post for when you're setting up (or cleaning up) your stack. And if you're not sure which tools fit your specific business type, a free assessment is linked in bio.
