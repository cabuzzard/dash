Privacy Policy

GS2020, doing business as Webguy Business Services · Last updated November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information GS2020, doing business as Webguy Business Services ("we", "us"), collects through webguyleadgeneration.com and our text-message (SMS) alerts, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit through our forms: your email address, phone or mobile number, and any note you choose to include.
  • SMS opt-in records: when you sign up for text alerts, we record the mobile number you entered, that you ticked the consent box, the consent wording you agreed to, the page you used, and the date and time.
  • Message content needed to deliver our SMS alerts, such as voicemail transcripts and booking details for calls to the business line.

How we use it

  • To respond to inquiries you send us and to provide the services you ask for.
  • To send the text alerts you opted in to, and to honor opt-out (STOP) and help (HELP) requests.
  • To keep records of consent as required by phone carriers and applicable law.

SMS opt-in data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your mobile number and consent are used only to send the alerts you signed up for. Text-message originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party, except with the service providers that deliver our messages (such as our SMS provider), and only as needed to deliver them.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. You can also email us to be removed.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run our website, forms, messaging and records (for example hosting, SMS delivery and database tools). They may process your information only to provide those services to us.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. We keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.
